# Tuberculosis · 2000-2024

Metric: Incidence of tuberculosis (per 100,000 people) (SH.TBS.INCD).
Definition: Incidence of tuberculosis is the estimated number of new and relapse tuberculosis cases arising in a given year, expressed as the rate per 100,000 population. All forms of TB are included, including cases in people living with HIV. Estimates for all years are recalculated as new information becomes available and techniques are refined, so they may differ from those published previously.
Original provider: Global Tuberculosis Report, World Health Organization (WHO)
Source: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.TBS.INCD

Top 10 reporting economies in each displayed year, considering all non-aggregate
World Bank country/economy records. Territories are included where the provider
reports them separately. Coverage ranges from 202 to 208 economies per year.
Missing observations are excluded for that year; they are never replaced with zero,
carried forward, or invented. This is a ranking of available records, not a claim
that every country has reported every year.

Period selection: the longest contiguous interval within the requested source period
with at least 156 reporting economies per year.
For automatically selected periods this is at least 75% of peak annual coverage,
with a topic minimum of 30. This avoids incomplete recent
updates being presented as a comparable complete ranking. Country inclusion is never
hand-picked. Excluded years and full annual coverage are recorded in the data audit.

Exact source values determine the order. Exact ties share a competition rank;
ISO3 code order selects/stabilizes the ten displayed rows at a tied cutoff.
Displayed values are rounded to 1 decimal places after dividing by
1. The metric definition and units above govern interpretation.
Positive values below display precision use a less-than label instead of a false zero.
The publisher’s indicator definition above governs interpretation.

Between published annual observations, bars and numbers glide by straight-line
interpolation so motion is continuous; each published year anchors exact values
at the segment ends. No observations outside the configured historical period and
no forecasts are added. The source may itself contain estimates or revisions;
provider definitions/observation flags are preserved in the source files and audit.
Current flags and current provider entity names serve as identifiers, not a reconstruction
of historical flags, borders, or political status.
The Channel Islands reporting unit has a neutral geographic icon, not an invented flag.

Verification: 5,157 nonmissing country/year observations
cross-checked between World Bank JSON and its separately downloaded bulk CSV;
zero mismatches. This checks faithful reproduction of the published dataset, not
independent remeasurement of the underlying economic/demographic estimates.
