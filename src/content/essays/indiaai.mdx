The Union Cabinet approved **₹10,371.92 crore** for the IndiaAI Mission in March 2024. By 9 February 2026, IndiaAI, the MeitY implementation arm, had received **₹400.94 crore**, according to a [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (Unstarred Question 1668, answered 13 February 2026). That is **3.9%** of the five-year outlay, 23 months into a 60-month plan. The same parliamentary reply disclosed that more than **38,000 GPUs** had been "onboarded." Empaneled silicon and released rupees are different objects, and the gap between them is the story of the Mission so far.

## The ledger has four levels

Mission funding passes through four distinct ledgers, and public debate mixes them freely.

1. **Outlay.** The Cabinet ceiling of ₹10,371.92 crore, fixed in March 2024.
2. **Estimate.** The annual Budget Estimates and Revised Estimates that MeitY actually carries.
3. **Release.** Cash that moves from the Ministry of Finance to IndiaAI, disclosed in parliamentary replies.
4. **Spend.** What IndiaAI then pays to providers, startups, and institutions. This ledger is not published pillar-wise.

The [13 February 2026 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) publishes levels 2 and 3 for IndiaAI as a whole. It publishes level 1 pillar-wise. It does not publish level 4 at all.

## ₹10,372 Cr on paper

The outlay split across seven pillars plus a 1% overhead line appears in the [February 2026 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) and in [MeitY's earlier Lok Sabha disclosure](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/).

| Pillar | Outlay (₹ Cr) | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compute Capacity | 4,563.36 | 44.0% |
| Foundation Models | 1,971.37 | 19.0% |
| Startup Financing | 1,942.50 | 18.7% |
| FutureSkills | 882.94 | 8.5% |
| Applications | 689.05 | 6.6% |
| Datasets (AIKosh) | 199.55 | 1.9% |
| Overheads | 102.69 | 1.0% |
| Safe & Trusted AI | 20.46 | 0.2% |
| **Total** | **10,371.92** | **100%** |

Source [Rajya Sabha Unstarred Q.1668](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf), answered 13 Feb 2026 (MeitY). Figures sum to ₹10,371.92 Cr.

Two observations. Safe & Trusted AI, the pillar that sells responsible AI, is allocated about one-fifth of the overhead line. And the sub-project breakdown inside Compute Capacity's ₹4,563.36 crore has never been published, as [MediaNama noted](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/) in February 2025.

## The release record

The estimates shrank before the releases underperformed. In FY 2024-25, the first year of the Mission, the Budget Estimate of [₹551.75 crore was cut 69% to a Revised Estimate of ₹173 crore](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). Against that reduced RE, IndiaAI received ₹21.79 crore.

| Year | BE (₹ Cr) | RE (₹ Cr) | Received (₹ Cr) | Share of RE | Share of BE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | 551.75 | 173.00 | 21.79 | 12.6% | 3.9% |
| 2025-26 | 2,000.00 | 800.00 | 379.15 | 47.4% | 19.0% |
| 2026-27 | 1,000.00 | n/a | 0.00 | 0% | 0% |
| **Total** | n/a | n/a | **400.94** | n/a | **3.9% of outlay** |

Sources [Rajya Sabha Unstarred Q.1668 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (13 Feb 2026) and [MediaNama, Feb 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). FY 2025-26 cut-off 9 Feb 2026. BE 2025-26 of ₹2,000 crore is from the [Union Budget documents](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Budget_at_Glance/budget_at_a_glance.pdf) as reported by MediaNama.

Read the table against the ceiling, not just the RE column. Against the original Budget Estimate, FY 2024-25 delivered **3.9%** of what MeitY asked for, and FY 2025-26 delivered **19%**. The five-year ceiling of ₹10,372 crore was never an annual cheque, but even the smaller estimates are running far ahead of the cash. [MediaNama published the same reading](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-indiaai-mission-400-crore-over-rs-10000-crore-5-year-outlay-released/) in April 2026.

A note on FY 2026-27. The Budget Estimate is ₹1,000 crore. The reply was cut off on 9 February 2026, before the first release of the new financial year, so the zero in the Received column is a cut-off artefact, not a full-year outcome.

## What the GPU count hides

IndiaAI does not principally buy and warehouse chips. It empanels cloud providers, routes approved users, and subsidises access. The [30 May 2025 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) published L1 bid prices from seven bidders, including Cyfuture, Netmagic, Sify, Locuz, Vensysco, Ishan Infotech, and Yotta.

| SKU | On-demand (₹/hr) | 1-month (₹/hr) | L1 bidder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA L4 (1X) | 45.07 | 29.00 | Sify / Netmagic |
| Intel Gaudi 2 (1X) | 57.60 | 46.80 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA L40S (1X) | 67.50 | 49.50 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA A100 80GB | 135.90 | 89.10 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA H200 SXM | 140.00 | 135.00 | Netmagic |
| NVIDIA H100 SXM | 153.00 | 134.10 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA B200 SXM | 323.00 | 308.00 | Yotta |

Source [PIB Press Release ID 2132817](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) (30 May 2025), Table 1.

The ₹65/hour slogan, repeated in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092), is real for entry-level SKUs on one-month pricing. Training-class silicon is not. An H100 SXM is **₹153/hour** on-demand and **₹134.10/hour** on one-month pricing. A B200 SXM is **₹323/hour**. The February 2025 tender round averaged [₹115.85 per GPU-hour](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/), which sits close to the L40S line, not the H100 line.

The GPU count itself is a layered funnel. [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms), citing an IndiaAI letter to providers, reported the following for committed compute.

| Stage | GPUs | Share of committed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Committed | 33,099 | 100% |
| Assigned | 12,638 | 38% |
| Utilised by end users | 7,418 | 22% |

Figures as reported by [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms), citing an IndiaAI letter to empanelled providers. Secondary until MeitY reprints the series in a primary annex.

The second tender round drew bids for 18,000 GPUs, with about 15,000 expected to clear technical checks, per [IndiaAI CEO Abhishek Singh in May 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/05/223-indiaai-mission-18000-gpu-bids-indigenous-ai-models/). The same report noted that Vensysco and Locuz were pairing with AWS, Ishan Infotech with Oracle, and Netmagic with Google Cloud. IndiaAI's national compute is largely rented hyperscaler capacity routed through Indian resellers.

The February 2026 parliamentary reply and the utilisation figures reported in 2026 point the same way.

## The Sarvam money trail

On 26 April 2025, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) said Sarvam AI was selected to build a sovereign open-source 120B-parameter stack. Twelve teams had been shortlisted for indigenous foundation models by the [February 2026 Rajya Sabha reply](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf), and in April 2026 [BharatGen was reported with over ₹1,000 crore](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-centre-funds-12-ai-projects-sovereign-models-bharatgen-4x-next-highest-allocation-rs-1000-crore/), four times the next-highest allocation among the twelve.

The Sarvam deal is the most consequential single instrument. [MediaNama, citing Economic Times](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/), reported the terms.

| Term | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Compute subsidy | ₹98.68 Cr |
| Total compute bill | ₹246.71 Cr |
| GPUs | 4,096 × NVIDIA H100 |
| Duration | 6 months |
| Instrument | Compulsorily convertible debentures |
| Potential conversion | 1-2% government stake |
| Sarvam Series B first close | $234M (June 2026) |
| Post-money valuation | ~$1.5B |

Press-reported via [Economic Times, as covered by MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/). The commercial terms are not in the Rajya Sabha PDF.

Two things stand out. First, a 40% subsidy structure is visible in the numbers, ₹98.68 of ₹246.71 crore, matching the [40% subsidy frame](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/) MeitY announced in January 2025. Second, the instrument is not a grant. If the CCD path holds, public compute subsidy becomes a residual ownership claim in a startup that closed a $234 million round in June 2026, days before [US export controls tightened access to Anthropic's frontier models](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-suspension-us-export-controls/). Some IndiaAI participants have [resisted equity-for-compute structures](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/indiaai-startups-resist-centres-plan-to-seek-equity-for-support/articleshow/126356889.cms) on dilution grounds. That debate became concrete in June 2026, when the conversion terms were reported.

Separately, Tamil Nadu signed a [₹10,000 crore MoU with Sarvam for a sovereign AI park](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-tamil-nadu-sarvam-ai-sovereign-ai-park/) in January 2026, roughly the size of the entire central Mission. As of mid-2026, public AI infrastructure in India runs on at least three ledgers, central Mission, state MoU, and private round.

## Thin edges of the same outlay

**AIKosh** (₹199.55 Cr allocated) reported over 3,000 datasets and 243 models in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092). Live counts move on the [AIKosh portal](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/). Count is not quality. [MediaNama has noted](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/) that MeitY has not published a scrub protocol matching the privacy risk of residual identifiers in "AI-ready" public datasets.

**FutureSkills** (₹882.94 Cr) lists more than 8,000 undergraduates, 5,000 postgraduates, 500 PhDs, 27 live labs, and 543 identified labs in the [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf). Enrolment is not labour-market outcome.

**Startup Financing** (₹1,942.5 Cr) nearly matches Foundation Models on paper. Public instrument-level disbursements are thinner than the GPU price annex. [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092) highlights a ten-startup Europe programme with Station F and HEC Paris. Ten startups do not absorb a ₹1,942 crore line.

**AIRAWAT** at C-DAC, the prior flagship AI platform, [suffered three months of downtime](https://www.medianama.com/2024/11/223-all-is-not-well-with-it-ministry-ai-platform-airawat/) in late 2024, disrupting startups that depended on it. **Bhashini** is another adjacent MeitY brand. Neither is a substitute for the IndiaAI cash line. Mixing their metrics with IndiaAI's onboarded GPUs double-counts national compute.

## Mechanism

Private operators finance and rack GPUs. IndiaAI buys routing and subsidy on top. [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms) describes a roughly 40% IndiaAI / 60% end-user cost split on utilisation, press-attributed. The [parliamentary annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) confirms an End User Policy naming startups, MSMEs, researchers, students, and government entities as eligible classes.

When utilisation lags, providers hold idle capex while the government reports a GPU count. Both sides can announce the next capacity target before publishing use of the last one. Amazon and Microsoft have committed [over $50 billion to India's cloud and AI infrastructure](https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-amazon-microsoft-52b-india-ai-investment/), dwarfing IndiaAI's received cash. Those are corporate balance sheets, not Mission rupees. Do not add them to the Mission ledger. Do notice who owns the racks, because the ₹10,372 crore Mission buys access to them, not ownership of them.

India's high-functioning digital programmes priced success in completed transactions, UPI payments and Aadhaar authentications, as argued in the [API States](/apis) and [bureaucracy](/bureaucracy) essays. As of the February 2026 reply, IndiaAI's public scoreboard prices success in onboarded GPUs and Cabinet outlays.

By early 2026, IndiaAI had received ₹401 crore against a ₹10,372 crore Cabinet ceiling, while public messaging led with 38,000+ onboarded GPUs whose utilisation Parliament did not prove at the same scale. The Mission can still ship models, labs, and subsidised hours. Until MeitY publishes releases and utilisation as often as it publishes GPU counts, most of the ₹10,371.92 crore remains unspent, and the count that moves fastest is the one on the press release.
