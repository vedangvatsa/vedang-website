The Union Cabinet approved **₹10,371.92 crore** for the IndiaAI Mission in March 2024. By 9 February 2026, IndiaAI, the MeitY implementation arm, had received **₹400.94 crore**, according to a [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (Unstarred Question 1668, answered 13 February 2026). That is **3.9%** of the five-year outlay, 23 months into a 60-month plan.

The same reply said more than **38,000 GPUs** had been "onboarded." Put the two numbers together and you get the shape of the Mission so far. The money released is 3.9% of the plan. The GPUs actually used by end users are about **22%** of the committed pool, per press reporting. The figure that keeps moving is the count on the press release.

This piece follows the money, the machines, and the one deal, Sarvam, that shows how the Mission actually works.

## The money: ₹401 crore in 23 months

Mission money sits in four separate ledgers. Public statements mix them up.

1. **Outlay.** The Cabinet ceiling of ₹10,371.92 crore, fixed in March 2024.
2. **Estimate.** The annual Budget Estimates and Revised Estimates that MeitY actually carries.
3. **Release.** Cash that moves from the Ministry of Finance to IndiaAI, disclosed in parliamentary replies.
4. **Spend.** What IndiaAI then pays to providers, startups, and institutions. This ledger is not published pillar-wise.

The [13 February 2026 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) publishes levels 2 and 3 for IndiaAI as a whole. It publishes level 1 pillar-wise. It does not publish level 4 at all.

| Year | BE (₹ Cr) | RE (₹ Cr) | Received (₹ Cr) | Share of RE | Share of BE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | 551.75 | 173.00 | 21.79 | 12.6% | 3.9% |
| 2025-26 | 2,000.00 | 800.00 | 379.15 | 47.4% | 19.0% |
| 2026-27 | 1,000.00 | n/a | 0.00 | 0% | 0% |
| **Total** | n/a | n/a | **400.94** | n/a | **3.9% of outlay** |

Sources [Rajya Sabha Unstarred Q.1668 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (13 Feb 2026) and [MediaNama, Feb 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). FY 2025-26 cut-off 9 Feb 2026. BE 2025-26 of ₹2,000 crore is from the [Union Budget documents](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Budget_at_Glance/budget_at_a_glance.pdf) as reported by MediaNama.

The pattern matters more than any single row. The Mission's annual ask has been cut every year since approval. FY 2024-25 was trimmed **69%**, from [₹551.75 crore to ₹173 crore](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). FY 2025-26 was trimmed **60%**, from ₹2,000 crore to ₹800 crore. FY 2026-27 asks for ₹1,000 crore, half of the previous year's request. The releases then ran even further behind the trimmed figures, 12.6% of RE in year one, 47.4% of RE in year two.

Against the original Budget Estimates, the picture is starker. FY 2024-25 delivered **3.9%** of what MeitY asked for. FY 2025-26 delivered **19%**. [MediaNama published the same cash reading](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-indiaai-mission-400-crore-over-rs-10000-crore-5-year-outlay-released/) in April 2026.

A note on FY 2026-27. The reply was cut off on 9 February 2026, before the first release of the new financial year, so the zero in the Received column reflects the cut-off date, not the full year.

The GPU number grew from 10,000 in the March 2024 Cabinet note to 34,333 empaneled in May 2025 to 38,000+ onboarded in October 2025. Cash received went from ₹21.79 crore to ₹379.15 crore in the same period.

Then put the two registers next to each other. Parliament's own beneficiary counts list **305 slots** for researchers, startups, students, and government bodies. The committed GPU pool is **33,099**. More than one hundred GPUs for every named user slot. Nobody publishes the two numbers on the same page, because the page would not read as a success story.

## The machines: empaneled, not owned

IndiaAI does not buy and store chips itself. It empanels cloud providers, approves users, and subsidises access. The [30 May 2025 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) published L1 bid prices from seven bidders, including Cyfuture, Netmagic, Sify, Locuz, Vensysco, Ishan Infotech, and Yotta.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA L4 (1X) | 45.07 | 29.00 | Sify / Netmagic |
| Intel Gaudi 2 (1X) | 57.60 | 46.80 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA L40S (1X) | 67.50 | 49.50 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA A100 80GB | 135.90 | 89.10 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA H200 SXM | 140.00 | 135.00 | Netmagic |
| NVIDIA H100 SXM | 153.00 | 134.10 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA B200 SXM | 323.00 | 308.00 | Yotta |

Source [PIB Press Release ID 2132817](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) (30 May 2025), Table 1.

The ₹65/hour figure, repeated in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092), applies to entry-level GPUs on one-month pricing. It does not apply to training-class GPUs. An H100 SXM is **₹153/hour** on-demand and **₹134.10/hour** on one-month pricing. A B200 SXM is **₹323/hour**. The February 2025 tender round averaged [₹115.85 per GPU-hour](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/).

The poster price, the cheapest month, the training-class price. Three different numbers, all official, all true, none of them the same thing. The Mission's own numbers work the same way, as the funnel below shows.

| Stage | GPUs | Share of committed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Committed | 33,099 | 100% |
| Assigned | 12,638 | 38% |
| Utilised by end users | 7,418 | 22% |

These figures come from the [Economic Times report](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms), which cites an IndiaAI letter to empanelled providers. Treat them as press-reported until MeitY publishes the same series itself.

Two of every three committed GPUs had not been assigned when the ET report was filed. Roughly one in five was in use. The government announced the next target, a [second tender round drawing bids for 18,000 GPUs](https://www.medianama.com/2025/05/223-indiaai-mission-18000-gpu-bids-indigenous-ai-models/), before it published utilisation of the first.

The same report that covered the second tender noted who the bidders partner with. Vensysco and Locuz with AWS. Ishan Infotech with Oracle. Netmagic with Google Cloud. IndiaAI's national compute is mostly rented from big cloud companies through Indian resellers. The Mission buys access, not machines.

## The deal that explains the Mission: Sarvam

On 26 April 2025, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) said Sarvam AI was selected to build a sovereign open-source 120B-parameter stack. Twelve teams had been shortlisted for indigenous foundation models by the [February 2026 Rajya Sabha reply](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf), and in April 2026 [BharatGen was reported with over ₹1,000 crore](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-centre-funds-12-ai-projects-sovereign-models-bharatgen-4x-next-highest-allocation-rs-1000-crore/), four times the next-highest allocation among the twelve.

Sarvam received the largest compute subsidy in the programme. [MediaNama, citing Economic Times](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/), reported the terms.

| Term | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Compute subsidy | ₹98.68 Cr |
| Total compute bill | ₹246.71 Cr |
| GPUs | 4,096 × NVIDIA H100 |
| Duration | 6 months |
| Instrument | Compulsorily convertible debentures |
| Potential conversion | 1-2% government stake |
| Sarvam Series B first close | $234M (June 2026) |
| Post-money valuation | ~$1.5B |

Press-reported via [Economic Times, as covered by MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/). The commercial terms are not in the Rajya Sabha PDF.

The subsidy is 40% of the bill, ₹98.68 crore of ₹246.71 crore, matching the [40% subsidy frame MeitY announced in January 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). The instrument is the part that changes the story. The support is not a grant. It is compulsorily convertible debentures, and if the CCDs convert, the government's compute subsidy becomes a small ownership stake in Sarvam.

Read that against the timing. Sarvam closed a $234 million round in June 2026, days before [US export controls limited access to Anthropic's frontier models](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-suspension-us-export-controls/). A sovereign-model bet looks different after export controls on foreign frontier models. It also looks different to the startups [who have said they do not want to give up equity in exchange for compute support](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/indiaai-startups-resist-centres-plan-to-seek-equity-for-support/articleshow/126356889.cms). The terms were reported in June 2026, which made that question concrete.

The same January 2026 window brought a [₹10,000 crore MoU between Tamil Nadu and Sarvam](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-tamil-nadu-sarvam-ai-sovereign-ai-park/) for a sovereign AI park, roughly the size of the entire central Mission. As of mid-2026, public AI infrastructure in India runs on at least three money flows, the central Mission, the state MoU, and private funding rounds.

## How the Mission actually works

Private operators buy and install the GPUs. IndiaAI pays to route users to them and subsidises the cost. [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms) describes a split of roughly 40% paid by IndiaAI and 60% by end users. That split is press-reported. The [parliamentary annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) confirms an End User Policy naming startups, MSMEs, researchers, students, and government entities as eligible classes.

The structure explains the numbers. When utilisation is low, providers sit on idle machines while the government reports a GPU count. Both sides can announce the next capacity target before publishing how much the last one was used. Amazon and Microsoft have committed [over $50 billion to India's cloud and AI infrastructure](https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-amazon-microsoft-52b-india-ai-investment/), far more than IndiaAI has received. That is company money, not Mission money. Do not add it to the Mission total. Do note who owns the machines, because the ₹10,372 crore Mission pays for access to them, not for ownership.

India's most successful digital programmes counted completed transactions, UPI payments and Aadhaar authentications, as argued in the [API States](/apis) and [bureaucracy](/bureaucracy) essays. As of the February 2026 reply, IndiaAI still counts onboarded GPUs and Cabinet outlays.

## The other pillars

**AIKosh** (₹199.55 Cr allocated) reported over 3,000 datasets and 243 models in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092). Live counts move on the [AIKosh portal](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/). Count is not quality. [MediaNama has noted](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/) that MeitY has not published a scrub protocol matching the privacy risk of residual identifiers in "AI-ready" public datasets.

**FutureSkills** (₹882.94 Cr) lists more than 8,000 undergraduates, 5,000 postgraduates, 500 PhDs, 27 live labs, and 543 identified labs in the [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf). Enrolment is not labour-market outcome.

**Startup Financing** (₹1,942.5 Cr) nearly matches Foundation Models on paper. Details of who actually received the money are thinner than the GPU price annex. [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092) highlights a ten-startup Europe programme with Station F and HEC Paris. Ten startups cannot absorb a ₹1,942 crore line.

**AIRAWAT** at C-DAC, the prior flagship AI platform, [suffered three months of downtime](https://www.medianama.com/2024/11/223-all-is-not-well-with-it-ministry-ai-platform-airawat/) in late 2024, disrupting startups that depended on it. **Bhashini** is another adjacent MeitY brand. Neither replaces the IndiaAI money. Adding their GPU numbers to IndiaAI's counts the national total twice.

## What would show the Mission working

Three observable things would move this piece forward.

1. **Release cadence.** Quarterly releases from MeitY to IndiaAI, published against the annual estimate, so the 3.9% figure has a current date.
2. **Hours used per rupee.** Utilisation in GPU-hours, not counts, published alongside subsidy rupees, so the money can be read against the machines.
3. **CCD terms.** The Sarvam conversion price, licence terms, and any converted equity percentage, in a primary document rather than press reporting.

The pillar-wise outlay table exists. The pillar-wise spend table does not. Until MeitY publishes releases and utilisation as often as it publishes GPU counts, the Mission's progress will be measured by the numbers it chooses to publish.

By early 2026, IndiaAI had received ₹401 crore of a ₹10,372 crore Cabinet outlay, while public messaging led with 38,000+ onboarded GPUs. The estimates shrank every year, the releases ran behind the estimates, and the utilisation ran behind the releases. The Mission can still ship models, labs, and subsidised hours. Until the scoreboard changes from counts to usage, most of the ₹10,371.92 crore remains unspent, and the number that moves fastest is the one on the press release.
