The [Union Cabinet](https://pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2012375) approved **₹10,371.92 crore** for the IndiaAI Mission in March 2024. By 9 February 2026, IndiaAI, the MeitY implementation arm, had received **₹400.94 crore**, according to a [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (Unstarred Question 1668, answered 13 February 2026). That is **3.9%** of the outlay.

<Figure src="/images/essays/indiaai.svg?v=6" alt="Ledger chart showing 10372 crore Cabinet outlay as a full bar and 401 crore received as a 3.9 percent slice" wide />

The same [parliamentary reply](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) that disclosed the cash also repeated the Mission's lead success metric. More than **38,000 GPUs** had been "onboarded." Empaneled silicon and released rupees are different objects. [Cabinet releases](https://pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2012375), [PIB tender tables](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817), and the Rajya Sabha annex dated 13 February 2026 separate them.

## Cash received vs the Cabinet ceiling

[MeitY told the Rajya Sabha](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) how much money IndiaAI had received.

<IndiaAIFundsReleased />

The five-year ceiling was never an annual cheque. Even against the much smaller revised estimates, FY 2024-25 delivered about one-eighth of the RE. FY 2025-26 was still open when the reply was cut off. [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-indiaai-mission-400-crore-over-rs-10000-crore-5-year-outlay-released/) published the same cash reading in April 2026.

## Where the ₹10,372 Cr was allocated on paper

The outlay split across seven pillars plus 1% overhead appears in the [February 2026 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) and in [MeitY's earlier Lok Sabha disclosure](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/).

<IndiaAIPillarAllocation />

Safe & Trusted AI is allocated about one-fifth of the overhead line. The annex that sells responsible AI also prices safety below contingency.

The reply does **not** publish pillar-wise expenditure. It publishes pillar-wise *outlays* and year-wise *cash received by IndiaAI*. Those are not the same ledger.

## "38,000 GPUs" means empaneled private cloud

IndiaAI does not principally buy and warehouse chips. It empanels cloud providers, routes approved users, and subsidises access. The [30 May 2025 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) published L1 bid prices from seven bidders, including Cyfuture, Netmagic, Sify, Locuz, Vensysco, Ishan Infotech, and Yotta.

<IndiaAIGPUPriceTable />

The ₹65/hour slogan, repeated in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092), is real for some entry-level SKUs on one-month pricing (L4, Gaudi 2, L40S). An H100 SXM at L1 is **₹153/hour** on-demand and **₹134.10/hour** on one-month pricing. A B200 SXM is **₹323/hour** on-demand, per the [same tender table](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817). Training-class silicon is not the ₹65 poster rate.

[The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms), citing an IndiaAI letter to providers, later reported a utilisation funnel on committed GPUs. **33,099** committed. **12,638** assigned (38%). **7,418** utilised by end users (22%). Treat those three figures as press-reported until MeitY reprints them in a primary annex.

<IndiaAIComputeFunnel />

Parliament's own user counts are in the [Q.1668 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf).

<IndiaAIUserCounts />

Hundreds of beneficiary slots sit next to a tens-of-thousands GPU headline. [Takshashila](https://legion.takshashila.org.in/research/state-of-ai-governance) had warned in 2025 that eligibility friction would leave Mission compute underused, as [MediaNama reported](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-indiaai-mission-compute-capacity-underused-takshashila-report/). The later numbers point the same way.

## The Sarvam money trail

On 26 April 2025, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) said Sarvam AI was selected to build a sovereign open-source 120B-parameter stack, with Soket, Gnani, and Gan also selected in the May announcement. By February 2026, MeitY said twelve teams had been shortlisted for indigenous foundation models in the [Rajya Sabha reply](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf).

Press reporting on Sarvam's compute support puts a figure on one deal. [MediaNama, citing Economic Times](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/), reported a subsidy of **₹98.68 crore** against a **₹246.71 crore** compute bill for **4,096 NVIDIA H100** GPUs over six months, structured as compulsorily convertible debentures that may convert into a **1-2%** government stake. Those commercial terms are **not** in the Rajya Sabha PDF. They stay labelled as reported until a primary instrument appears.

If the CCD path holds, public compute subsidy becomes a residual ownership claim. That is a different accountability surface than a pure grant. Hours used, licence terms, and eval scores then matter as much as the selection press release.

## Thin edges of the same outlay

**AIKosh** (₹199.55 Cr allocated) published catalogue metrics in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092). Over 3,000 datasets and 243 models by July 2025. Live counts move on the [AIKosh portal](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/). Count is not quality. [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/) noted that MeitY has not published a scrub protocol matching the privacy risk of residual identifiers in "AI-ready" public datasets.

**FutureSkills** (₹882.94 Cr) lists enrolment targets in the [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf). More than 8,000 undergraduates, 5,000 postgraduates, 500 PhDs, 27 labs live, 543 identified. Enrolment is not labour-market outcome.

**Startup Financing** (₹1,942.5 Cr) nearly matches Foundation Models on paper. Public instrument-level disbursements are thinner than the GPU price annex. [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092) highlights a ten-startup Europe programme with Station F and HEC Paris. Ten startups do not absorb a ₹1,942 crore line.

**[AIRAWAT](https://airawat-psai.cdac.in/airawat/)** at C-DAC and **[Bhashini](https://bhashini.gov.in/)** are adjacent MeitY brands. They are not substitutes for the IndiaAI cash line. Mixing their metrics with IndiaAI's onboarded GPUs double-counts national compute.

## Mechanism

Private operators finance and rack GPUs. IndiaAI buys routing and subsidy on top. [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms) describes a ~40% IndiaAI / ~60% end-user cost split on utilisation. That split is press-attributed. The [parliamentary annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) confirms an End User Policy and names eligible classes (startups, MSMEs, researchers, students, government entities).

When utilisation lags, providers hold idle capex and the government still holds a GPU count. Both sides can announce the next capacity target before publishing use of the last one. Amazon and Microsoft India cloud and AI commitments, discussed in tens of billions of dollars in [MediaNama's reading of the same reply](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-indiaai-mission-400-crore-over-rs-10000-crore-5-year-outlay-released/), dwarf IndiaAI's received cash. Do not add corporate announcements into the Mission ledger. Do notice who owns the racks.

India's high-functioning digital programmes priced success in completed transactions (UPI payments, Aadhaar authentications), as argued in the [API States](/apis) and [bureaucracy](/bureaucracy) essays. IndiaAI's public scoreboard still prices success in onboarded GPUs and Cabinet outlays.

<KeyTakeaway>
By early 2026, IndiaAI had received ₹401 crore against a ₹10,372 crore Cabinet ceiling, while public messaging led with 38,000+ onboarded GPUs whose utilisation Parliament did not prove at the same scale.
</KeyTakeaway>

The Mission can still ship models, labs, and subsidised hours. Until MeitY publishes releases and utilisation as often as it publishes GPU counts, most of the ₹10,371.92 crore remains unspent.
