The Union Cabinet approved an outlay of ₹10,371.92 crore for the IndiaAI Mission on [7 March 2024](https://pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2012375). By 9 February 2026, IndiaAI, an Independent Business Division under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and the mission's primary implementing agency, had received ₹400.94 crore in fiscal disbursements. This corresponds to 3.9% of the five-year outlay, 23 months into the 60-month mission horizon ([Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question 1668](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf), answered 13 February 2026).

While public announcements noted that over 38,000 GPUs had been "onboarded," vendor empanelment lists represent registered cloud capacity pools rather than immediate direct capital expenditures.

## Budgetary framework and fiscal ledgers

Financial tracking for the mission spans four distinct fiscal accounting categories:

1. **Cabinet Outlay**: The five-year statutory ceiling of ₹10,371.92 crore approved in March 2024, defining the multi-year program boundary.
2. **Annual Estimates**: The Budget Estimate (BE) presented at the start of each fiscal year and subsequent Revised Estimates (RE) set mid-year.
3. **Fiscal Receipts**: Actual funds transferred from the consolidated revenue accounts to IndiaAI.
4. **Program Expenditure**: Direct payments disbursed by IndiaAI to empaneled cloud providers, research consortia, and academic institutions.

Parliamentary disclosures have detailed overall estimates and receipts for IndiaAI alongside pillar-level outlay ceilings, while granular pillar-by-pillar expenditure breakdowns remain unpublished.

Compute Capacity represents the largest single program allocation at ₹4,563.36 crore (44% of total outlay), while the Safe & Trusted AI pillar accounts for ₹20.46 crore (0.2%).

## Year-by-year budgetary adjustments

In both completed financial years, mid-year Revised Estimates were adjusted downward from initial Budget Estimates, with actual disbursements following the revised targets:

<Figure src="/images/essays/indiaai-cascade.svg?v=5" alt="IndiaAI cash: year 1 asked 551.75 crore, received 21.79 crore. Year 2 asked 2,000 crore, received 379.15 crore." source="Union Budget BE; Rajya Sabha Q.1668 for RE and cash received" sourceUrl="https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf" width={800} height={220} wide />

- **FY 2024–25**: Budget Estimate was reduced by 69% from ₹551.75 crore to ₹173.00 crore, with actual disbursements reaching ₹21.79 crore (3.9% of opening BE).
- **FY 2025–26**: Budget Estimate was revised down 60% from ₹2,000.00 crore to ₹800.00 crore, with ₹379.15 crore disbursed as of 9 February 2026.
- **FY 2026–27**: Union Budget proposed a Budget Estimate of ₹1,000.00 crore.

Potential factors contributing to these budget adjustments include phased multi-stage procurement schedules, lower-than-projected per-hour cloud compute tender bids, and inter-departmental budget reallocations.

Empanelment tallies (such as 45,000+ GPUs) measure aggregate cloud capacity registered across commercial partners under multi-tenant rental arrangements, which operate independently of actual compute hours billed.

## Compute procurement and pricing structure

The IndiaAI compute architecture is structured as a subsidized rental marketplace. IndiaAI empanels Cloud Service Providers (CSPs), verifies eligible research and enterprise projects, and covers a portion of the hourly compute cost.

The [30 May 2025 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) published lowest-bid (L1) price discovery rates across participating vendors (including Cyfuture, Netmagic, Sify, Locuz, Vensysco, Ishan Infotech, and Yotta):

<p class="table-source">Rates in ₹ per hour. Source: <a href="https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817">PIB 2132817</a>, Table 1.</p>

Effective user pricing varies significantly by hardware tier: an NVIDIA L4 on a monthly commitment costs ₹29/hour, whereas an on-demand NVIDIA B200 costs ₹323/hour. Consequently, blended rate figures (such as the advertised ₹65/hour subsidy) reflect weighted administrative averages rather than a uniform single price point.

According to reporting by [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms) on internal agency documentation, compute pipeline progression across early phases was structured as follows:

| Committed Capacity | 33,099 | 100.0% |

In [Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 2807](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2807_hKyI1X.pdf) (5 August 2026), MeitY reported that 15 compute providers had been empaneled across four tender rounds, 237 projects had been approved, and 93.18 lakh GPU hours had been sanctioned. These figures quantify aggregate project approvals rather than cumulative machine hours consumed.

## Foundation model support and financing mechanisms

Under the Foundation Models pillar (outlay of ₹1,971.37 crore), MeitY received 506 proposals by April 2025 and shortlisted 12 consortia for foundational development ([Lok Sabha Question 5417](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/loksabhaquestions_annex_187_AU5417_haoHcv.pdf), March 2026).

Among approved initiatives:
- **BharatGen** (an IIT Bombay-led multi-institutional consortium): ₹1,058.52 crore in total compute and development support.
- **Sarvam AI**: ₹246.72 crore in compute package valuation.

Terms reported by [The Economic Times and MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/) for Sarvam AI's compute allocation include:

<div class="table-wrapper">

| Structural Term | Reported Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Compute Subsidy Allocation | ₹98.68 Cr (40% subsidy rate) |
| Total Compute Package Valuation | ₹246.71 Cr |
| Hardware Allocation | 4,096 × NVIDIA H100 GPUs |

The use of convertible debentures introduces an equity-linked structure where public subsidies can convert into minority state equity upon specific funding thresholds, reflecting a shared upside model alongside traditional public research grants.

## Program deliverables across pillars

1. **Foundational Models**: By August 2026, MeitY reported 20 indigenous models selected for support (12 large multimodal models and 8 small language models), with intellectual property retained by the developers. Deployed outputs listed by the ministry include Sarvam AI's models (30B and 105B), Gnani.AI's speech-to-speech system, BharatGen's Param2-17B and Patram-7B, and Avataar AI's video generation models.
2. **Sectoral Applications**: 30 initial application proposals were approved across healthcare, agriculture, climate adaptation, and assistive learning. Following the February 2026 AI Impact Summit, 762 departmental problem statements were catalogued, leading to 62 public-sector prototypes and 20 pilot deployments.
3. **Centres of Excellence**: 58 AI Centres of Excellence have received formal approvals, with 22 initiated across 13 States and Union Territories.
4. **AIKosh Data Repository**: AIKosh expanded from 367 datasets in May 2025 to over 14,000 datasets and 331 open models by July 2026.
5. **FutureSkills and Talent**: The ministry reported awarding 686 fellowships across 178 institutions and approving 543 Data and AI Labs at ITIs and polytechnics.
6. **Safe & Trusted AI**: 13 responsible AI research initiatives have been funded under the Safe & Trusted AI pillar, alongside the formulation of national AI governance guidelines.

## Enterprise scale and infrastructure metrics

Private technology investments in India operate at a substantially larger capital scale than the central government mission budget. Multi-year commitments announced by Amazon ($35+ billion by 2030) and Microsoft ($17.5 billion over 2026–2029) encompass broader enterprise cloud services, logistics networks, and data center real estate alongside dedicated AI hardware.

While transactional digital public infrastructure (such as UPI payment settlement or Aadhaar authentication) is benchmarked on daily completed volume, current IndiaAI indicators primarily measure capacity empanelment, project authorizations, and compute hours sanctioned.
