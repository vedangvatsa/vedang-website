The Union Cabinet approved **₹10,371.92 crore** for the IndiaAI Mission in March 2024. By 9 February 2026, IndiaAI, the MeitY implementation arm, had received **₹400.94 crore**, according to a [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (Unstarred Question 1668, answered 13 February 2026). That is **3.9%** of the five-year outlay, 23 months into a 60-month plan. The same parliamentary reply disclosed that more than **38,000 GPUs** had been "onboarded." Empaneling GPUs and releasing money are two different things. This piece looks at the difference between them.

## The ledger has four levels

Mission money sits in four separate ledgers. Public statements mix them up.

1. **Outlay.** The Cabinet ceiling of ₹10,371.92 crore, fixed in March 2024.
2. **Estimate.** The annual Budget Estimates and Revised Estimates that MeitY actually carries.
3. **Release.** Cash that moves from the Ministry of Finance to IndiaAI, disclosed in parliamentary replies.
4. **Spend.** What IndiaAI then pays to providers, startups, and institutions. This ledger is not published pillar-wise.

The [13 February 2026 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) publishes levels 2 and 3 for IndiaAI as a whole. It publishes level 1 pillar-wise. It does not publish level 4 at all.

## ₹10,372 Cr on paper

The outlay is split across seven pillars plus a 1% overhead line. The split appears in the [February 2026 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) and in [MeitY's earlier Lok Sabha disclosure](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/).

| Pillar | Outlay (₹ Cr) | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compute Capacity | 4,563.36 | 44.0% |
| Foundation Models | 1,971.37 | 19.0% |
| Startup Financing | 1,942.50 | 18.7% |
| FutureSkills | 882.94 | 8.5% |
| Applications | 689.05 | 6.6% |
| Datasets (AIKosh) | 199.55 | 1.9% |
| Overheads | 102.69 | 1.0% |
| Safe & Trusted AI | 20.46 | 0.2% |
| **Total** | **10,371.92** | **100%** |

Source [Rajya Sabha Unstarred Q.1668](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf), answered 13 Feb 2026 (MeitY). Figures sum to ₹10,371.92 Cr.

Two points. Safe & Trusted AI, the pillar for responsible AI, gets about one-fifth of the overhead line. And the sub-project breakdown inside Compute Capacity's ₹4,563.36 crore has never been published, as [MediaNama noted](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/) in February 2025.

## The release record

The annual estimates were cut, and the cash released was even smaller. In FY 2024-25, the first year of the Mission, the Budget Estimate of [₹551.75 crore was cut 69% to a Revised Estimate of ₹173 crore](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). Against that reduced RE, IndiaAI received ₹21.79 crore.

| Year | BE (₹ Cr) | RE (₹ Cr) | Received (₹ Cr) | Share of RE | Share of BE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-25 | 551.75 | 173.00 | 21.79 | 12.6% | 3.9% |
| 2025-26 | 2,000.00 | 800.00 | 379.15 | 47.4% | 19.0% |
| 2026-27 | 1,000.00 | n/a | 0.00 | 0% | 0% |
| **Total** | n/a | n/a | **400.94** | n/a | **3.9% of outlay** |

Sources [Rajya Sabha Unstarred Q.1668 annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) (13 Feb 2026) and [MediaNama, Feb 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/). FY 2025-26 cut-off 9 Feb 2026. BE 2025-26 of ₹2,000 crore is from the [Union Budget documents](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Budget_at_Glance/budget_at_a_glance.pdf) as reported by MediaNama.

Compare the table to the ceiling, not only to the RE column. Against the original Budget Estimate, FY 2024-25 delivered **3.9%** of what MeitY asked for, and FY 2025-26 delivered **19%**. The five-year ceiling of ₹10,372 crore was never meant to be released in one year, but even the reduced estimates are far above the cash released. [MediaNama published the same reading](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-indiaai-mission-400-crore-over-rs-10000-crore-5-year-outlay-released/) in April 2026.

A note on FY 2026-27. The Budget Estimate is ₹1,000 crore. The reply was cut off on 9 February 2026, before the first release of the new financial year, so the zero in the Received column reflects the cut-off date, not the full year.

## The GPU numbers

IndiaAI does not buy and store chips itself. It empanels cloud providers, approves users, and subsidises access. The [30 May 2025 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) published L1 bid prices from seven bidders, including Cyfuture, Netmagic, Sify, Locuz, Vensysco, Ishan Infotech, and Yotta.

| SKU | On-demand (₹/hr) | 1-month (₹/hr) | L1 bidder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA L4 (1X) | 45.07 | 29.00 | Sify / Netmagic |
| Intel Gaudi 2 (1X) | 57.60 | 46.80 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA L40S (1X) | 67.50 | 49.50 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA A100 80GB | 135.90 | 89.10 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA H200 SXM | 140.00 | 135.00 | Netmagic |
| NVIDIA H100 SXM | 153.00 | 134.10 | CyFuture |
| NVIDIA B200 SXM | 323.00 | 308.00 | Yotta |

Source [PIB Press Release ID 2132817](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) (30 May 2025), Table 1.

The ₹65/hour figure, repeated in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092), applies to entry-level GPUs on one-month pricing. It does not apply to training-class GPUs. An H100 SXM is **₹153/hour** on-demand and **₹134.10/hour** on one-month pricing. A B200 SXM is **₹323/hour**. The February 2025 tender round averaged [₹115.85 per GPU-hour](https://www.medianama.com/2025/02/223-indiaai-mission-allocation-reduced-69-percent-2024-2025-estimates/).

GPUs pass through several stages before anyone uses them. [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms), citing an IndiaAI letter to providers, reported these numbers.

| Stage | GPUs | Share of committed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Committed | 33,099 | 100% |
| Assigned | 12,638 | 38% |
| Utilised by end users | 7,418 | 22% |

These figures come from the [Economic Times report](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms), which cites an IndiaAI letter to empanelled providers. Treat them as press-reported until MeitY publishes the same series itself.

The second tender round drew bids for 18,000 GPUs. About 15,000 were expected to clear technical checks, per [IndiaAI CEO Abhishek Singh in May 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/05/223-indiaai-mission-18000-gpu-bids-indigenous-ai-models/). The same report noted that Vensysco and Locuz were pairing with AWS, Ishan Infotech with Oracle, and Netmagic with Google Cloud. IndiaAI's national compute is mostly rented from big cloud companies through Indian resellers.

Parliament also got user counts. They are listed below.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Researchers | 114 | 37% |
| Government | 58 | 19% |
| Startups & MSMEs | 47 | 15% |

Hundreds of user slots against tens of thousands of onboarded GPUs. [Takshashila warned in April 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-indiaai-mission-compute-capacity-underused-takshashila-report/) that strict eligibility rules and slow processes would leave Mission compute underused. It ranked India last among the US, China, and the EU for AI governance transparency. The February 2026 parliamentary reply and the 2026 utilisation figures point the same way.

## The Sarvam money trail

On 26 April 2025, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2132817) said Sarvam AI was selected to build a sovereign open-source 120B-parameter stack. Twelve teams had been shortlisted for indigenous foundation models by the [February 2026 Rajya Sabha reply](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf), and in April 2026 [BharatGen was reported with over ₹1,000 crore](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-centre-funds-12-ai-projects-sovereign-models-bharatgen-4x-next-highest-allocation-rs-1000-crore/), four times the next-highest allocation among the twelve.

The Sarvam deal is the largest single deal so far. [MediaNama, citing Economic Times](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/), reported the terms.

| Term | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Compute subsidy | ₹98.68 Cr |
| Total compute bill | ₹246.71 Cr |
| GPUs | 4,096 × NVIDIA H100 |
| Duration | 6 months |
| Instrument | Compulsorily convertible debentures |
| Potential conversion | 1-2% government stake |
| Sarvam Series B first close | $234M (June 2026) |
| Post-money valuation | ~$1.5B |

Press-reported via [Economic Times, as covered by MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/). The commercial terms are not in the Rajya Sabha PDF.

Two points. First, the numbers match the 40% subsidy that MeitY announced in January 2025, ₹98.68 crore of a ₹246.71 crore bill. Second, the support is not a grant. If the CCDs convert, the government's compute subsidy becomes a small ownership stake in Sarvam. Sarvam closed a $234 million round in June 2026, days before [US export controls limited access to Anthropic's frontier models](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-anthropic-fable-5-mythos-5-suspension-us-export-controls/). Some IndiaAI participants have [said they do not want to give up equity in exchange for compute support](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/indiaai-startups-resist-centres-plan-to-seek-equity-for-support/articleshow/126356889.cms). The terms were reported in June 2026, which made that question concrete.

Separately, in January 2026 Tamil Nadu signed a [₹10,000 crore MoU with Sarvam](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-tamil-nadu-sarvam-ai-sovereign-ai-park/) for a sovereign AI park. That is roughly the size of the whole central Mission. As of mid-2026, public AI infrastructure in India runs on at least three money flows, the central Mission, the state MoU, and private funding rounds.

## The other pillars

**AIKosh** (₹199.55 Cr allocated) reported over 3,000 datasets and 243 models in the [October 2025 PIB explainer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092). Live counts move on the [AIKosh portal](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/). Count is not quality. [MediaNama has noted](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-indiaai-mission-expenditure-budget/) that MeitY has not published a scrub protocol matching the privacy risk of residual identifiers in "AI-ready" public datasets.

**FutureSkills** (₹882.94 Cr) lists more than 8,000 undergraduates, 5,000 postgraduates, 500 PhDs, 27 live labs, and 543 identified labs in the [Rajya Sabha annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf). Enrolment is not labour-market outcome.

**Startup Financing** (₹1,942.5 Cr) nearly matches Foundation Models on paper. Details of who actually received the money are thinner than the GPU price annex. [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2178092) highlights a ten-startup Europe programme with Station F and HEC Paris. Ten startups cannot absorb a ₹1,942 crore line.

**AIRAWAT** at C-DAC, the prior flagship AI platform, [suffered three months of downtime](https://www.medianama.com/2024/11/223-all-is-not-well-with-it-ministry-ai-platform-airawat/) in late 2024, disrupting startups that depended on it. **Bhashini** is another adjacent MeitY brand. Neither replaces the IndiaAI money. Adding their GPU numbers to IndiaAI's counts the national total twice.

## How the money flows

Private operators buy and install the GPUs. IndiaAI pays to route users to them and subsidises the cost. [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/underused-gpus-raise-questions-about-indiaai-capacity-build-out/articleshow/128981840.cms) describes a split of roughly 40% paid by IndiaAI and 60% by end users. That split is press-reported. The [parliamentary annex](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/annex_270_AU1668_ajohnN.pdf) confirms an End User Policy naming startups, MSMEs, researchers, students, and government entities as eligible classes.

When utilisation is low, providers sit on idle machines while the government reports a GPU count. Both sides can announce the next capacity target before publishing how much the last one was used. Amazon and Microsoft have committed [over $50 billion to India's cloud and AI infrastructure](https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-amazon-microsoft-52b-india-ai-investment/), far more than IndiaAI has received. That is company money, not Mission money. Do not add it to the Mission total. Do note who owns the machines, because the ₹10,372 crore Mission pays for access to them, not for ownership.

India's most successful digital programmes counted completed transactions, UPI payments and Aadhaar authentications, as argued in the [API States](/apis) and [bureaucracy](/bureaucracy) essays. As of the February 2026 reply, IndiaAI still counts onboarded GPUs and Cabinet outlays.

The Mission can still ship models, labs, and subsidised hours. Until MeitY publishes releases and utilisation as often as it publishes GPU counts, most of the ₹10,371.92 crore remains unspent.
